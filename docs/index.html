Terms, risks and what this is

Not affiliated with WHOOP. "WHOOP" is a trademark of WHOOP, Inc., used here only to say which hardware this works with. This project is independent, contains no WHOOP code, firmware or keys, and is not endorsed by them.

What it does. It reads measurements your own device takes from your own body. It bypasses no subscription or payment, and gives access to no account and to nobody else's data.

No warranty, no liability. Provided "as is" under the MIT Licence. To the fullest extent permitted by law the authors accept no liability for any loss or damage — including damage to your device, loss of data, or loss of warranty cover — arising from using it. You use it entirely at your own risk.

Risks you accept. Third-party software may void your device warranty. Connecting from a computer ends its pairing with the WHOOP app, which you can restore by pairing there again. This page sends only a short allowlist of read and session-toggle commands — it cannot erase storage, reboot the device, change its saved configuration or write firmware. That is a safeguard taken in good faith, not a guarantee.

Not a medical device. Not certified by any regulator. Nothing here is a diagnosis or medical advice, and beat intervals come from an optical pulse sensor, not an ECG. Do not use it to make a health decision — ask a clinician.

Your data. Readings stay in this browser and are never uploaded; the authors never see them and hold no backup. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so export anything you want to keep.

This browser can't reach Bluetooth. Web Bluetooth works in Chrome, Edge, Opera and Brave on macOS, Windows, Linux and Android. Safari and Firefox don't support it, and on iPhone or iPad no browser does — try Bluefy there.
On iPhone, use Bluefy. Apple ships no Bluetooth support in the browser, and every iOS browser is Safari underneath — so Safari, Chrome and any in-app browser all stop here. Bluefy brings its own, and it is free.
Get Bluefy
Then paste the link into Bluefy's address bar. Everything else on this page — including the sample-data run below — works here as it is.
One connection at a time. Bluetooth lets a strap talk to a single device, so it needs to be free before it can reach this page. Connecting here also ends its pairing with the WHOOP app — pair it again there whenever you want to go back.
  1. Wear the strap and free it up — close the WHOOP app on your phone, or turn that phone's Bluetooth off.
  2. Press Connect a strap and pick your WHOOP in the browser's chooser.
  3. Readings start within seconds, and stay on this device.

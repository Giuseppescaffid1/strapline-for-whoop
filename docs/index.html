Your strap. Your data.

Connects straight to a WHOOP 4.0 over Bluetooth and shows live heart rate and variability. Nothing is uploaded — the readings never leave this browser.

This browser can't reach Bluetooth. Web Bluetooth works in Chrome, Edge, Opera and Brave on macOS, Windows, Linux and Android. Safari and Firefox don't support it, and on iPhone or iPad no browser does — try Bluefy there.
Quit the WHOOP app on your phone first. A strap talks to one device at a time, and connecting here ends its pairing with the phone — you'll have to pair it again in the WHOOP app afterwards.
  1. Wear the strap and close the WHOOP app on your phone.
  2. Press Connect a strap and pick your WHOOP in the browser's chooser.
  3. Readings start within a few seconds. Everything stays on this device.

Independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by WHOOP. Only reads data and toggles the live stream — it can't erase, reboot or reconfigure a strap.
